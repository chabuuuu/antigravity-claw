# Usage & Model Quotas Dashboard

**Antigravity OpenClaw** features an integrated, real-time **Usage & Model Quotas** monitoring system that bridges directly to the Google Antigravity CLI quota engine (`agy -p "/usage" --output-format json` and `/quota`).

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Overview

Google Antigravity allocates model quota based on tier and token compute costs. Because developers rely on multiple models across long-running autonomous sessions, knowing your exact remaining capacity and reset countdown prevents unexpected task interruptions.

OpenClaw tracks:
1. **Gemini Models** (Gemini Flash, Gemini Pro).
2. **Claude and GPT Models** (Claude Opus, Claude Sonnet, GPT-OSS).
3. **Gemini 4 Argon** (Dedicated Pro/Ultra capacity).
4. **Aggregate Token Analytics** (Prompt, Completion, Thinking tokens across tasks).

---

## 📊 Quota Groups & Limits Explained

### 1. Gemini Models
- **Models within this group**: `Gemini Flash`, `Gemini Pro`
- **Weekly Limit Remaining**: Your weekly quota tied directly to your individual Google account tier.
- **5-Hour Limit Remaining**: A rolling 5-hour window designed to smooth out aggregate demand and fairly distribute global capacity.
- **Reset Countdown**: Shows human-readable reset duration (e.g., `Refreshes in 140h 35m` or `Refreshes in 4h 47m`).

### 2. Claude and GPT Models
- **Models within this group**: `Claude Opus`, `Claude Sonnet`, `GPT-OSS`
- Models share weekly and 5-hour limits calculated proportionally to third-party model token costs.

### 3. Gemini 4 Argon
- For paid Pro plans, Argon quota is separate. Higher Argon quota (included as part of the larger Gemini quota bucket) is available on Ultra plans.

## ⚡ How to Check Quota in OpenClaw

### Method A: Sidebar Dashboard View
1. Open the left sidebar and click **`📊 Usage & Quotas`**.
2. View your signed-in Google account email (`id_token_email`).
3. Inspect visual progress bars with dynamic color coding:
   - 🟢 **Green / Blue** (> 50% remaining)
   - 🟡 **Yellow / Amber** (20% - 50% remaining)
   - 🔴 **Red** (< 20% remaining)
4. Click **🔄 Refresh Quotas** for an instantaneous update.

### Method B: Top Header Quota Pill
- The top header navbar displays a live compact quota badge:
  `📊 Gemini: 85% · Claude: 100%`
- Clicking this badge switches directly to the full Usage & Quotas view.

### Method C: In-Chat `/usage` and `/quota` Slash Commands
You can type `/usage` or `/quota` in the chat input at any time. OpenClaw intercepts the command client-side, queries the quota endpoint, and renders graphical quota cards directly inside the conversation stream.

## 📈 Token Analytics

At the bottom of the Usage & Quotas view, OpenClaw aggregates token usage data stored across completed tasks in the SQLite database:
- **Total Tasks Executed**: Count of autonomous runs completed.
- **Total Tokens**: Cumulative tokens processed across all model calls.
- **Prompt Tokens**: Input tokens fed into models.
- **Completion & Output Tokens**: Tokens generated by models.
- **Thinking Tokens**: Special reasoning tokens consumed during extended deliberation (`--effort high/xhigh/max`).
